## 1 About this policy

WolfPack Management & Information Services, LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company doing business as CommuniBetter ("CommuniBetter," "we," "us," or "our"), provides a community-engagement platform. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how long we retain it, and how you can contact us about your information.

This policy covers https://CommuniBetter.com, our platform, hosted communities and organization pages, and services that link to this policy (the "Services"). Available features include accounts, membership management, communications, meetings, surveys, polls, elections, volunteer activities, donations, and optional AI-assisted functionality. Information collected depends on the features actually available, enabled, and used. This policy describes the practices for the Services as they become available. As of October 5, 2026, CommuniBetter is prelaunch and has no platform users or user-information records. The categories below describe information handled when the corresponding feature is used; they do not imply that every category has already been collected.

"Personal information" means information that identifies, relates to, or can reasonably be linked to an individual, including equivalent terms under applicable law. "Participating organizations" means organizations using the Services, such as churches, nonprofits, homeowners associations, businesses, educational institutions, civic or political organizations, and governmental entities. "Organization data" includes records and content processed on an organization's behalf and may include personal information.

This policy applies to visitors, account holders, organization representatives, participants, people contacting us, and individuals whose information an organization lawfully submits, even if those individuals never create an account. Read it with our Terms of Service and any applicable supplemental privacy notice. A more specific supplemental notice governs the processing it addresses. No provision limits a right or protection that applicable law makes nonwaivable.

## 3 Information we collect and its sources

We receive information directly from you, from participating organizations and their authorized representatives, automatically through use of the Services, and from providers supporting the Services. We also generate records and outputs through platform activity. We do not collect every category from every person.

Account and profile information. Account registration currently uses email and requires a name, username, and email address. Depending on the feature and information provided, we may receive telephone numbers, addresses, profile images, age-related information, organization affiliations, roles, preferences, invitation records, and authentication or recovery information. Passwords are stored in hashed form. We do not currently offer social or single-sign-on registration through Google, Apple, Microsoft, or Facebook. Standard accounts do not require government-issued identification, Social Security numbers, biometrics, or formal Know Your Customer documentation.

Organization and participation information. We receive organization names, contact details, branding, settings, administrator information, member or directory records, roles and permissions, and records of meeting, survey, election, event, volunteer, or other participation. An organization may provide information about someone who does not have an account.

Communications and submitted content. We process messages, announcements, posts, comments, photographs, documents, attachments, forms, survey or poll responses, meeting responses, and other content submitted through available features. We also receive support inquiries and feedback. Related records may include submission dates, senders and recipients, organization associations, delivery status, and technical metadata. Content can include sensitive information depending on what people submit.

Donation records. We currently provide a link to a PayPal-hosted donation page. PayPal collects payment and financial information directly under its own terms and privacy notice. We do not receive or store complete card numbers, card-verification codes, financial-account login credentials, or PINs entered there. We may receive donor names, contact details, donation amounts and dates, payment status, transaction identifiers, and related receipt, refund, dispute, tax, or accounting records. These records may be maintained outside our application database. We do not currently process subscriptions or other in-application transactions through PayPal or another processor.

Technical and usage information. Our systems and infrastructure may collect IP addresses, browser and device information, access times, session and authentication information, pages or features used, referring URLs, application and server logs, errors, diagnostics, and security-event information. Approximate location may be inferred from an IP address where supported by our infrastructure. Addresses or event locations may also be voluntarily provided. We do not currently collect precise device location through GPS, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi scanning, or background location tracking.

AI-related information. When optional AI is enabled for a live listening meeting, we process response text, prompts, relevant context, classifications, analyses, summaries, and associated meeting or technical information. Section 7 explains the provider and information flow.

Sensitive information. Organization affiliation or submitted content may reveal religious or political views, health information, or other characteristics protected by particular laws. Account credentials and some communications or financial information may also be sensitive. We limit processing to authorized purposes and provide consent or other protections where required. We do not sell sensitive information or disclose it for targeted advertising. Where applicable law requires opt-in consent for sensitive-data processing, we obtain the legally required consent before that processing. An organization enabling a feature does not substitute for an individual's consent where individual consent is required.

Information you should not submit. Unless an expressly authorized feature requests it for a lawful purpose, do not submit Social Security numbers, complete payment-card information, financial-account credentials, government identification documents, confidential medical records, children's information, or other highly sensitive or restricted information. We do not intentionally request biometric identifiers, genetic information, or precise device geolocation. We may restrict, remove, or otherwise lawfully address prohibited submissions.

The Services are not designated for processing protected health information on behalf of a HIPAA-covered entity or business associate unless we expressly agree in writing, designate the appropriate service, and enter any required business associate agreement. Health-related content is not necessarily HIPAA-regulated merely because it concerns health.

## 6 Cookies and browser technologies

We use limited first-party authentication, session, security, and functional technologies. Browser localStorage holds JWT access and refresh tokens and certain meeting-participant identifiers. Depending on the authenticated or administrative flow, our Django framework may use first-party session or CSRF-related technologies. Stored information remains until it expires, is replaced or removed through the application, or is cleared through browser settings.

We do not currently use a third-party web analytics provider, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, web beacons, or third-party advertising software development kits. First-party meeting-result charts and exports use information in our systems rather than advertising or cross-site tracking technology.

You can clear or block cookies and website storage through browser or device controls. Doing so may sign you out, remove saved identifiers, or interfere with features. PayPal or another independent service may use its own cookies when you interact with it; its own disclosures and controls apply.

We do not currently provide a cookie-consent banner or preference-management tool. Before introducing nonessential technologies requiring consent or another choice, we will provide the required disclosure and control. The need for a control depends on the technology and applicable law, including similar technologies beyond cookies.

We do not use tracking technologies that enable advertisers or analytics companies to collect personal information about your activity over time and across unrelated websites while you use CommuniBetter. Ordinary Do Not Track signals do not change our current collection practices. We recognize legally required opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control where applicable. Because we do not sell information or share it for targeted advertising, such a signal may not change our present practices. When you leave CommuniBetter to use PayPal or another independent service, that service may collect information and use tracking technologies under its own privacy notice.

## 7 AI and automated processing

We currently use the OpenAI API for optional classification and analysis of responses during certain live listening meetings. AI is enabled meeting by meeting, not across all platform activity. When enabled, response text, prompts, and relevant context are sent to OpenAI, and generated classifications, analyses, or summaries return to our systems. General organization-profile and account data are not transmitted for this feature. Personal details voluntarily included in response text may nevertheless be included in the submitted material.

Under our current configuration, API data sharing for model improvement, evaluation and fine-tuning data sharing, and Playground feedback sharing are disabled. OpenAI may retain API information in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days under this configuration and may process information to provide services, maintain security, detect abuse, or comply with legal obligations under its agreement with us. Our retention of responses and outputs is separate and described in Section 8.

We do not currently train or fine-tune a model owned or independently developed by CommuniBetter using personal information, submitted content, organization data, prompts, or outputs. We do not authorize providers to use personal information submitted through these features to train or improve general-purpose models unless first clearly disclosed and lawfully authorized.

AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete, or biased. Organizations and users should review outputs and verify material facts before relying on them. AI is not a substitute for professional advice. We do not currently use AI as the sole basis for decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects. Operational automation also supports authentication, permissions, technical diagnostics, and requested platform functions. Applicable rights concerning qualifying profiling or automated decisions are described in Section 10.

## 8 Retention and disposal

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary and proportionate for the disclosed purpose, lawful organization instructions, our agreements, and legal obligations. Retention varies by category and circumstances rather than using a single fixed period for all records. We consider sensitivity, the relationship or transaction involved, recordkeeping duties, applicable limitation periods, security needs, and technical deletion processes. We do not retain information merely because it might someday be useful.

Account information is retained as needed to administer the active account. Closure ends active use but may leave records necessary for pending transactions, support, security, legal compliance, or disputes. Not every record must remain for the account's entire lifetime. Where available, account controls let you update profile information or initiate account deletion; you may also contact us using Section 13.

Organization data, including meeting responses, participation records, and AI outputs, is retained according to the organization's lawful instructions, available settings, our agreement, platform-level practices, and applicable law. Leaving an organization or closing an individual account does not automatically delete the organization's records. Organizations may request an authorized export or return at termination under available functionality and their agreement. Remaining data is disposed of when the applicable service, transition, retention, or legal need ends.

Donation and transaction records are retained as needed for accounting, tax, reconciliation, refund, dispute, and legal requirements. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve inquiries, document assistance, address recurring issues, and handle related legal claims. Technical and security logs are retained as needed for troubleshooting, service integrity, fraud prevention, investigation, and compliance.

Daily automatic Cloud SQL backups may temporarily retain copies removed from active systems. Backup and archived copies are deleted, overwritten, or made inaccessible through the applicable retention processes, subject to legal holds. If deleted information is restored, we take reasonable steps to reapply the deletion or restriction unless retention is lawfully required. Our current recovery practice uses the most recently available backup or save point where practicable; we do not currently maintain a formal disaster-recovery program or guarantee complete recovery.

Legal holds may extend retention only for information reasonably related to the matter. When the hold ends, ordinary retention and disposal rules resume unless another lawful need remains. We limit access to retained copies. When information is no longer needed, we use appropriate deletion, secure destruction, return, or legally sufficient anonymization or de-identification, considering the records and systems involved. Independent services such as PayPal retain their own records under their policies and legal duties.
